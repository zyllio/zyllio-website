Frequently asked questions

Your questions about the program.

Another question? Our team replies within 24 business hours.

By sending your application, you confirm that you have read and accept the affiliate program terms.

How much does it cost to join?

Nothing. Joining the affiliate program is free.

Who can become an affiliate?

Agencies, freelancers, trainers, non-profits and professional communities.

How are my referrals tracked?

Automatically. Your client signs up with your personal link, or enters your affiliate code when signing up. Your link and code are available at any time in your Zyllio account.

When do I get paid?

Every month, by bank transfer, upon receipt of your invoice.

How is this different from the Agency partner program?

Affiliation pays you for recommending Zyllio. The Agency partner program is for agencies that build their clients’ applications with Zyllio.
