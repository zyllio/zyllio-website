Zyllio Affiliate Program

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Overview

Zyllio is a no-code SaaS platform for designing, deploying and managing web and mobile applications without technical development.

The Zyllio Affiliate Program enables freelancers, agencies and partners to recommend Zyllio and earn a commission on generated subscriptions.

2. Definitions

“Lead” means any person or company referred to Zyllio by the Partner.

“Qualified Customer” means a Lead who has subscribed to a paid Zyllio offer through the Partner.

“Eligible Revenue” means the net-of-tax amounts actually paid by customers to Zyllio for subscriptions, excluding refunds, discounts and third-party services.

“Commission” means the percentage applied to Eligible Revenue generated by Qualified Customers.

“Affiliate Code” means the unique identifier assigned to the Partner and used by Leads when registering to track conversions.

3. Purpose

These terms set out the conditions under which the Partner may promote Zyllio and receive a commission on sales generated through its activities.

4. Status of the parties

The Partner acts as an independent contractor. No employment, commercial agency or joint venture relationship is created between Zyllio and the Partner. The Partner may not bind Zyllio contractually.

5. Joining the program

The Partner may promote Zyllio to its customers or audience, share its Affiliate Code and recommend Zyllio as part of its services or advisory work.

The Affiliate Code must be entered by the customer when registering or communicated to Zyllio when the introduction is made. Zyllio may accept or refuse any application to join the program.

6. Commissions

  • The Partner receives a 20% commission on Eligible Revenue, being net-of-tax amounts actually paid by customers.
  • The commission continues without time limit while the customer remains active and generates revenue.

Commissions are calculated only on net-of-tax amounts collected by Zyllio. No commission is due in the event of a customer refund, fraud or customer cancellation.

Example: if a customer subscribes for €120 including VAT, comprising €100 excluding VAT and €20 VAT, the commission is calculated on €100. The Partner therefore receives €20, or 20% of that amount, while the subscription remains active.

7. Lead validation

A Lead is valid if it is not already a Zyllio customer, is not already in commercial discussions with Zyllio, and uses the Partner's Affiliate Code during registration or is otherwise identified by Zyllio as referred by that Partner.

Zyllio may accept or reject a Lead. If several partners claim the same customer, Zyllio awards the commission to the partner that introduced the Lead first, based on available information such as the code used, contact date and commercial communications.

8. Payment

Commissions are paid monthly, within 30 days after the end of each month.

Payment requirements

To receive commissions, the Partner must be able to issue an invoice compliant with applicable law. The Partner is solely responsible for its administrative, tax and social-security status and must have a status enabling it to invoice its services.

No commission may be paid without a valid invoice. Zyllio may suspend or refuse payment if the documents supplied are non-compliant.

9. Partner obligations

The Partner undertakes to promote Zyllio honestly and transparently, respect Zyllio's brand and reputation, and refrain from abusive practices such as spam or deception.

The Partner must not use the Zyllio brand in advertising campaigns without authorisation, create misleading content, or manipulate affiliate tracking through fraud or abusive redirects.

10. Intellectual property

Zyllio remains the sole owner of its brand, product and marketing materials.

11. Liability

Zyllio shall not be liable for indirect losses or damage connected with the Partner's activities.

12. Confidentiality

The Partner must keep confidential all non-public information relating to Zyllio, including information about its customers, strategy, product and pricing.

13. Term and termination

The program is entered into for an indefinite term. Either party may end it at any time with 30 days' notice.

Zyllio may immediately suspend or terminate the Partner's account if these terms are breached.

14. Governing law

These terms are governed by French law.
