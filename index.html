Help center
Can I change what the AI created?

Yes. The NoCode Studio shows you every screen, table and automation. You edit them by hand or by asking the AI, without rebuilding your application.

Can I use Zyllio from ChatGPT, Mistral or Claude?

Yes. The Zyllio agent connects to your AI assistant through the MCP protocol: you create screens and components, manage your data and publish your application right from the conversation. The ChatGPT plugin is public and approved by OpenAI.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Zyllio is designed for your business teams, with no technical skills required. If you want to move faster, our experts can also build your application for you.

What kinds of applications can I build?

Zyllio is general-purpose: field service, customer management, inventories, field forms, portals, case tracking, internal processes… You start from your business needs.

Where are my applications hosted?

In France, with the French hosting provider OVHcloud. Zyllio handles the hosting, security and technical maintenance of the platform.

Do my applications work on mobile?

Yes. A single application adapts to desktop, tablet and smartphone, for your team and your customers alike.

Can I publish on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes, as an option on eligible plans. By default, your applications are instantly available via link or QR code, with immediate updates and no app store commission.

Is there a commitment?

No. The Discovery plan is free with no time limit and no credit card. Paid plans have no minimum term.
