Frequently asked questions

Your questions about the partnership.

Another question? Our team replies within 24 business hours.

Who pays for the client application’s subscription?

Your client. To run an application in production, each client subscribes to the Zyllio plan that fits their needs: data volume, storage, integrations, automations or support level.

Can I set my own prices for my services?

Yes. You stay in control of your client relationship: you set your own prices for design, deployment and maintenance services.

How am I paid for the clients I bring in?

You earn recurring revenue on the subscriptions of the clients you bring in. The terms are presented to you when the partnership is approved.

How many seats are included?

Up to 5 agency seats are included. Each additional seat costs €19 excl. VAT per month.
