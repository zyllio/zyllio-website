Help center
Is the Discovery plan really free?

Yes, with no time limit and no credit card. You build and test your application, then move to a paid plan when you need it.

Is there a minimum term?

No. Paid plans are monthly with no commitment. You can change plans or cancel your subscription at any time, free of charge.

Do we get an invoice?

Yes, an invoice is issued for every payment. For annual billing or bank transfer payment, contact our team.

What happens if we reach a limit?

Your application keeps working, but only part of your data is displayed. Simply upgrade to the next plan to lift the limit.

Can we publish on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes, as a paid add-on on paid plans. By default, your applications are available via link or QR code, with immediate updates and no app store commission.

Can you build the application for us?

Yes. With Zyllio Services, our experts scope your needs, then design and deliver your application. You can then keep improving it yourself.
